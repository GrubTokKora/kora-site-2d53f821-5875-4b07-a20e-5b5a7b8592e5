Work in India

Painted in India,
honoured on stage.

JayaKrishna Bandari is a painter and educator from Andhra Pradesh, India. His practice began in the mid-1980s and grew through portrait commissions, gallery exhibitions, teaching and cinema: his canvases have been painted into Indian cinema, honored on stage, and exhibited across India and the United States.
